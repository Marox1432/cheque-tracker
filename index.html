Setup and alerts

1. Link your sheet

In Google Sheets choose File, Share, Publish to web. Pick the "Cheque Tracker" tab, choose CSV, publish, then paste the link here. The link stays on this device and is never stored in the app's code.

2. Alerts on this device

The first device creates the alert keys. Every other device pastes the same public key.

Save these two values as GitHub secrets now. The private key is shown only once and must stay secret.

3. Add cheques from the app

Set up the Google Apps Script once (steps are in the README), then paste its web app link and your secret word here. This stays on this device.

Dates follow the cheque date. UAE weekend is Saturday and Sunday.

Add a cheque

It is saved to your Cheque Tracker sheet and gets its alerts automatically.

The app is not linked to the add-cheque script yet. Link it once in Setup, under "3. Add cheques from the app".
